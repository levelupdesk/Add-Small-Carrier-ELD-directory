# Small-carrier ELD directory

A starting-point directory of electronic logging device (ELD) providers for small U.S. motor carriers. This directory lists ELD vendors, not motor carriers.

## Scope and inclusion

“Small carrier” is used here as a practical audience description for owner-operators and carriers operating 1–20 power units; it is not a legal or regulatory classification. The directory is intended for U.S. carriers that may need an ELD under the federal hours-of-service rules. Applicability and exceptions depend on the carrier's circumstances; consult FMCSA guidance or a qualified compliance professional.

A provider can be listed when it:

- Offers an ELD product for U.S. commercial motor carriers.
- Has a product that can be matched to an entry on FMCSA’s current registered ELD list.
- Publishes a provider website and a way to contact the company.

FMCSA registration is device-specific and can change. A provider’s marketing claim, an older directory, or a model from the same vendor is not enough to establish that a device is currently registered. FMCSA does not endorse or certify listed products. Verify the exact device and identifier on the [FMCSA Registered ELDs list](https://eld.fmcsa.dot.gov/List) before purchase or use.

## Contributing and maintaining listings

Open an issue or pull request to suggest a provider or update a listing. Include:

- Provider name, product name, and the exact FMCSA-listed device name and ELD identifier.
- The FMCSA Registered ELDs list entry and direct provider product/contact sources.
- U.S. service area, public contact details, and pricing only when a current primary source supports it.
- The date each detail was checked.

Check the official FMCSA list for the exact device immediately before adding or refreshing a listing. Remove or mark a listing unverified if its device is missing, revoked, or cannot be matched. Recheck entries periodically and whenever a reader reports a change. Do not describe an ELD as “FMCSA certified” or imply that inclusion is an FMCSA endorsement.
